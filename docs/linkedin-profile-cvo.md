## 4. About Section

The data-center buildout is pulling more power through local grids than many utilities can deliver on schedule. Utility-scale inverters, medium-voltage transformers and high-voltage interconnections are sitting in multi-year queues.

At Earth Protection Society LLC, we got ahead of that bottleneck a different way.

We call them **Electricity Power Harvesters**.

They harvest cheap off-peak electricity from the grid — typically 11 PM to 7 AM — store it in safe, silent LiFePO4 battery systems, and discharge it when rates spike or the grid falters. No combustion. No exhaust pipe. No multi-year interconnection queue.

We have developed three form factors:

• **Cart** — portable 2–5 kWh silent power for small sites, mobile crews and events.
• **Pod** — 30–80 kWh detached vaults for homes, small businesses and commercial buildings.
• **Container-scale plant** — 100–500+ kWh behind-the-meter peak shaving and backup for data centers and edge compute sites.

Every project is customer-funded. Every plant is owned by the site and the neighborhood that built it. Every technician is trained and certified locally.

If you are expanding compute capacity and the utility queue is your bottleneck, that is the conversation I want to have.

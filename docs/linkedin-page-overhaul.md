## 2. About section

Earth Protection Society is a community-owned technology and energy company building the sovereign power layer for neighborhoods, small businesses, and edge compute sites.

Growing Globally From The Block. When the world runs out of resources, we will always be here — food, soil, water, clean energy and transportation stay on the block.

We design, install, and service detached LiFePO4 battery vaults, silent mobile generator carts, towable power trailers, and container-scale energy plants. Every system runs on the same idea: buy electricity when it is cheap, store it, and use it when the grid is expensive or unreliable. No fumes. No noise. No multi-year interconnection queue.

Source globally, build locally. We qualify cells and inverters across multiple regions so one strike, fire, bankruptcy or tariff shock can never strand a neighborhood project — then every vault, pod and container is welded, wired, owned and serviced by certified block technicians.

What we are not: a venture-backed startup, a leasing company, or a remote utility program. Every project is customer-funded. Every plant is owned by the site and the neighborhood that built it.

Call 404-454-0602 for a same-day site evaluation, or apply to the Energy Corps at earthresonancehub.com/join.
